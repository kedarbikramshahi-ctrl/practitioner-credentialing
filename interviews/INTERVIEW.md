**Renata Kowalczyk** · Voice · 10:12 PM

If a source doesn't respond, I log up to three requests. Silence isn't a pass; the item stays outstanding. If information doesn't match the declaration, I send a discrepancy list to the applicant, who must resolve it in writing or by amending. I never choose between values; the applicant provides the resolution.
